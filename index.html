Luna Borgo — Portfolio

Hi, I'm Luna シ A designer focused on brand expression and scalable digital experiences. I build systems for teams that need to design at scale without losing their soul.

For over twelve years, I've helped brands to turn creative vision into real experiences while building shared design languages that brings teams together.

Heineken

I am a founding member of Crate, Heineken's global B2B design system. I built everything from tokens and components to workflows and, most recently, AI automations. With over 235 contributors in 8 global product teams, and organic adoption by OpCos, Crate has become the vital link within Heineken's digital product network.

Next to Crate, I also led the creation of the brand framework and team culture that launched Eazle, Heineken's global B2B platform consolidating 40 fragmented regional platforms into one cohesive product.

I established the design principles and visual language guidance for the brand application, and built collaboration rituals that enabled rapid scaling while keeping a strong design culture.

  • Community engagement up 230% in two years; component health hit 94%, and SUS stayed at 70, above the 68 industry standard.

About me

Design has been part of my life long before it became my career. Back when computers were still a three-piece setup, a passion for reading and writing led me to start a blog – only to become completely obsessed with how it looked... and with all the gifs I wanted to make.

Twelve years later, my obsession with pixels hasn't gone anywhere. Today, I channel it into making design more sustainable – and more fun. With a systems mindset and a passion for craft, I move fluidly between strategy and hands-on, making sure what we imagine is actually something we can ship.

By combine storytelling, visual craft and genuine interest, I inspire and connect. Helping brands scale while creating the conditions for teams to do their best work.

Away from screens, I'm still a person: I shoot on film, listen to loud music, read a bunch of books while drinking a bunch of coffee, maintain an ever-evolving Japan itinerary I may or may not act on and enjoy living in Amsterdam with a husband (whom I've already converted into a designer) and two cats (still working on those).
