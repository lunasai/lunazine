Luna Borgo — Portfolio

Hi, I’m Luna — I build systems for teams that need to design at scale without losing their soul.

For over twelve years, I’ve helped brands turn creative vision into real experiences while building shared design languages that bring teams together.

Recent work

Heineken

I joined Heineken in 2024, when we merged 40 regional B2B platforms into a unified global product ecosystem. This major organizational transformation required a shared language that was flexible enough to scale across diverse markets. In that scenario, I founded crate – Heineken’s global B2B design system. Shaping it from design tokens and workflows to, most recently, AI automations. I also led the brand application track and design culture, setting up frameworks and rituals that connected product, engineering, marketing and brand.

I enabled rapid scaling from 6 to over 245 contributors in two years, serving the three global Heineken products while keeping a strong design culture. Beyond that, local market teams in Brazil, the UK, and Vietnam adopted Crate to build their own products — without any mandate to do so.

  • 55% → 94% component health in 6 months
  • 40 regional platforms → 1 global B2B platform
  • Voluntary adoption across 4 local Heineken markets
  • 230% community engagement growth

Adidas

Before that, at Adidas, I partnered with data science and engineering to design a unified product recommendation framework. What started as a quick experiment grew into a cross-team effort to build a reusable component and recommendation framework that blends smart algorithms with editorial input.

We replaced 10+ scattered carousels with one shared component, supporting 36 variations and driving significant yearly revenue.

  • 10+ scattered carousels → 1 shared component

What I say

Design has been part of my life long before it became my career. Back when computers were still a multi-piece setup, a passion for reading and writing led me to start a blog – only to become completely obsessed with how it looked (and with all the gifs I wanted to make). Turns out that obsession never went away – it just grew up into design systems, tokens, and the exciting world of how brands communicate at scale.

Away from screens, I’m a person who shoots on film, listens to loud music, reads a bunch of books while drinking a bunch of coffee, maintains an ever-evolving Japan itinerary I may or may not act on, and enjoys living in Amsterdam with a husband (whom I’ve already converted into a designer) and two cats (still working on those).
